The Flanker Experiment

Instructions

In each trial, five arrows appear. Identify the direction of the centre arrow and ignore the others.

  • Press the LEFT arrow key if the centre arrow points left.
  • Press the RIGHT arrow key if the centre arrow points right.
  • A + appears first. Keep your eyes on it.
  • Respond as quickly and accurately as you can.
  • There are 10 trials. You will not see feedback.

Press the LEFT or RIGHT arrow key.

Experiment Complete

    Reaction times use correct responses only. Flanker effect = mean incongruent RT minus mean congruent RT.
